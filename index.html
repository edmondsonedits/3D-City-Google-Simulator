Google 3D Driving Test

Prototype

This test streams Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles through CesiumJS, then runs your own fire-truck physics and road intelligence on top. Version 0.0.2 adds a structured validation report for the Codex handoff.

The key is kept only in this browser tab (sessionStorage) and is sent directly to Google by Cesium. It is never committed to GitHub.
